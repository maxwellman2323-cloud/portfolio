## Site-Wide Trends as Evidence of LLM-Assisted Fiction Writing on Scribblehub 
*Summer Research Grant, Truman State University, Summer 2026*
*Advisor: Wayne Johnson*

**Summary:**

Scrapped and analyzed data from popular writing website "Scribblehub" on community engagement such as ratings, and style as measured by relative word frequency. I found that there was a sudden 43% increase in the number of fictions written, which was not consistent across different genres. Several factors point to the theory that this is caused by increased LLM usage, but this study was unable to confirm it. 
[Read the paper](assets/GIASR-Paper.pdf) 

## Rent Control Website
As a school project, students were required to collect messy data from multiple sources, clean it up, and use it to tell a story. I decided to do mine on rent control policies, because I had heard that they were not particularly effective, but did not trust the source very much. I collected data from various government and non government sources on rent control policies and housing prices, and created a variety of graphs and  models that showed that rent control did not appear to reduce housing prices. [Read more here](https://sites.google.com/truman.edu/data-520-project/home)
